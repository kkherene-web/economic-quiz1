Imagine that you have 1,000 dinars.
You want to buy food, a book, and a new phone.
But you cannot buy everything.
Your money is limited, so you must make a choice.
This is an economic situation.
Key idea
Economics is about choices when resources are limited.
Resources
Simple meaning: things that people, businesses, or countries can use
Example: Money, time, workers, and natural resources are important resources.
الموارد
Scarcity
Simple meaning: a situation where resources are limited
Example: Scarcity means that we cannot have everything we want.
الندرة
Consumer
Simple meaning: a person who buys or uses goods and services
Example: A consumer buys food from a shop.
المستهلك
Producer
Simple meaning: a person or company that makes goods or provides services
Example: A producer makes products for consumers.
المنتِج
Economics is the study of how people and organizations use limited resources to make choices.

Topic 2 • Why Is English Important?

English in Economics and Business: Three Reasons

English is presented through a future situation.

1. Read

English helps you read economic news and business information.
You can read reports, articles, and information about companies.
You can learn about economic events in different countries.

2. Communicate

English helps you communicate with people from other countries.
You may write emails, talk to customers, or work with international companies.
English can be useful in academic and professional situations.

3. Learn

English gives you access to more learning resources.
You can watch economic videos and use international websites.
You can continue learning about economics and business in English.
English is not only a language subject. It is also a tool for studying, communicating, and working in economics and business.
Topic 3 • Economics, Business and Management

One Company, Three Perspectives

We use one simple company to understand the difference between the three fields.
🏭
A company
makes products

Economics

Economics looks at how resources are used.
The company has limited money, workers, materials, and time.
Economics asks: How can these resources be used?

Business

Business is about producing and selling goods or services.
The company makes products and sells them to customers.
Business asks: What can the company produce and sell?

Management

Management is about planning and organizing people and resources.
Managers plan work and organize employees and resources.
Management asks: How can we organize the company?
Economics studies resource use. Business focuses on producing and selling. Management focuses on organizing people and resources.
Topic 4 • Economy, Economics and Economic

Three Similar Words

These words look similar, but they have different grammatical roles and meanings.
Economy
The economy is the economic system or activity of a country.

Economics
Economics is the study of how resources are used.

Economic
Economic is an adjective. It describes something related to the economy or economics.

Topic 5 • Microeconomics and Macroeconomics

Zoom In — Zoom Out

Think of economics like a camera. Microeconomics zooms in. Macroeconomics zooms out.
🔍
MICRO
Small parts
↔
🌍
MACRO
Big picture

Microeconomics — Zoom In

Microeconomics studies small parts of the economy.
It looks at consumers, businesses, products, prices, and markets.
For example, it can study why the price of one product changes.

Macroeconomics — Zoom Out

Macroeconomics studies the economy as a whole.
It looks at inflation, unemployment, economic growth, and the national economy.
For example, it can study why prices are increasing in a country.
Microeconomics looks at smaller parts of the economy. Macroeconomics looks at the economy as a whole.
